# Salesforce CRM Basics

## What is CRM
CRM (Customer Relationship Management) is a system used to manage customer information, sales, communication, and business workflows.

---

## Why Companies Use Salesforce
- Manage customer data
- Track sales
- Improve customer relationships
- Automate business processes
- Increase productivity

---

## Account
Represents a company or organization.

## Contact
Represents a person associated with the account.

## Opportunity
Represents a potential business deal.

---

## Business Flow
Lead → Contact → Opportunity → Customer
Lead = A person who shows interest in a product or service.
Contact = A verified person whose details are stored in the system.
Opportunity = A potential deal or business process being tracked.
Customer = A person or company that successfully completed the purchase or process.


## Real-World Mapping

### College Admission System

Account = College  
Contact = Student  
Lead = Student Inquiry  
Opportunity = Admission Process
